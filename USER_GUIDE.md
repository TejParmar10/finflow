# FinFlow — Your Personal Finance App 💸
### A guide for everyone (no tech knowledge needed!)

**🌐 Open the app:** https://finflow-six-gamma.vercel.app

---

## What is FinFlow?
FinFlow is your AI-powered money manager. It helps you:
- Track what you spend every day
- Plan how to divide your salary (savings vs personal use)
- Split bills with friends and get paid back easily
- Import your bank/GPay statements automatically
- Get a smart AI report on your finances every month

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign In
- Open the app → Click **"Continue with Google"**
- Use your Google / Gmail account — no new password needed

### 2. First-Time Setup (takes 30 seconds)
After signing in, a setup screen appears:

**Step 1 — Enter your monthly salary**
Move the slider or type your take-home salary (after taxes).

**Step 2 — Choose your split**
The slider lets you decide how much goes to:
- 🟢 **Invest** — savings, FDs, mutual funds
- 🔴 **Personal** — everyday spending (food, travel, shopping)

> 💡 **Tip:** A good starting point is 30% invest / 70% personal.

Click **"Get Started"** — your budget is ready!

### 🎯 Tracker
This is where you log your daily expenses.

**How to add an expense manually:**
1. Enter the ₹ amount
2. Write what it was for (e.g., "Lunch at Zomato")
3. Pick a category (Food, Transport, Entertainment, etc.)
4. Set the date → Click **"Add Expense"**

**Receipt Scanner (AI magic ✨):**
1. Click **"Scan Receipt"**
2. Take a photo of your paper receipt or upload an image
3. AI reads the receipt and lists all the items
4. Tick/untick what you want to add → Click **"Add All to Tracker"**

**Week tabs (Week 1 / 2 / 3 / 4):**
Switch between weeks to see your spending for that week only.

**Category Cards:**
Each category (Food, Transport, etc.) shows:
- How much you've spent vs the suggested limit
- A coloured bar (🟢 green = good, 🟡 yellow = careful, 🔴 red = over limit)
- A smart tip to help you save more

> 💡 Expenses imported from your bank statement show a small **"Imported"** tag so you know where they came from.

### 📤 Import Statement
Automatically pull in all your transactions from your bank or Google Pay.

**Supported sources:**
- ✅ HDFC Bank (PDF or CSV)
- ✅ Google Pay (PDF or CSV)

**How to import:**
1. Go to **Import Statement**
2. Select the month the statement belongs to
3. Drag & drop your file (or click to browse) — max 10 MB
4. Click **"Upload & Parse"**
5. AI reads your statement (may take 10–15 seconds)
6. A table appears with all your transactions
   - ✅ Tick/untick what you want to import
   - You can edit the category or description for any row
   - Duplicate transactions are flagged with ⚠️ and auto-unchecked
7. Click **"Import X Transactions"**

**Where to download your statements:**
- **Google Pay:** GPay app → Profile → Transaction history → Download PDF/CSV
- **HDFC Bank:** NetBanking → Accounts → Account Statement → Download

> 💡 Your raw PDF/CSV file is **never stored** on our servers. Only the transaction data is saved.

### 🎯 Goals
Set savings targets and track your progress.

**Creating a goal:**
1. Enter the goal name (e.g., "Goa Trip", "Emergency Fund")
2. Set the target amount (₹)
3. Pick a deadline date
4. Choose a category (House / Vehicle / Travel / Education / Emergency / Other)
5. Click **"Create Goal"** — the app calculates your weekly target automatically

**Tracking progress:**
- Each goal shows a progress bar and how many weeks are left
- Click **"Add Progress"** to log money you've set aside
- When you reach 100% — 🎉 celebration animation!

> 💡 **Weekly Target** is calculated as: Total Amount ÷ Number of Weeks until deadline. Try to add progress every week!

### 📄 Report
Get a full AI-written financial report for any month.

**How to generate:**
1. Select the month from the dropdown
2. Click **"Generate AI Report"**
3. Wait ~10 seconds while AI analyses your data
4. The report shows:
   - Your **Financial Health Score** for that month
   - An AI **summary** of your finances
   - ✅ **What you did well** (green section)
   - ❌ **Areas to improve** (red section)
   - 📊 **Spending breakdown** by category
   - 💡 **3 actionable recommendations** for next month

**Download as PDF:**
Click **"Download PDF"** to save a clean, printable report to your device.

### 👥 Splits
Split expenses with friends and family — and get paid back easily.

**Creating a group:**
1. Go to **Splits** → Click **"New Group"**
2. Give it a name (e.g., "Goa Trip 2026")
3. Pick a category: Trip / Home / Food / Other
4. Add members:
   - Search by email to find FinFlow users (they get a 🟢 "On FinFlow" badge)
   - Add anyone by name even if they don't use FinFlow
5. Click **"Create Group"**

**Adding an expense:**
1. Open the group → Click **"Add Expense"**
2. Fill in: description, amount (₹), who paid, category, date
3. Choose how to split:
   - **Equal** — divide equally among all members
   - **By %** — each person pays a different percentage
   - **By ₹** — enter exact amounts for each person
4. Click **"Add Expense"**

> 💡 When you add a group expense, FinFlow **automatically** adds your share to your personal Tracker too — so everything stays in sync!

**Settling up:**

*On mobile (Android/iPhone):*
- Click **"Settle"** next to a person
- Choose GPay / PhonePe / Paytm — it opens the payment app with the amount pre-filled
- After paying, click **"Mark as Paid"**

*On computer/laptop:*
- A QR code appears — scan it with any UPI app
- Or copy the UPI ID and pay manually
- Click **"Mark as Paid"** when done

**Simplify Debts:**
If your group has complicated back-and-forth payments, click **"Simplify Debts"** — AI calculates the minimum number of payments needed to settle everything.

**Inviting someone:**
If a member isn't on FinFlow yet, a yellow banner appears with a **"Copy Invite Link"** button. Share that link with them!

## Tips to Get the Most Out of FinFlow

| Habit | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Log expenses daily (takes 30 sec) | Accurate weekly + monthly reports |
| Import your bank statement monthly | Zero manual entry for big transactions |
| Set a goal for one big purchase | Weekly targets keep you on track |
| Check Insights every week | Catch overspending before it's too late |
| Use Splits for every group trip/meal | Never do mental maths again |
| Generate a report at month-end | Understand your patterns over time |

## Something Not Working?

- **Sign-in issues** → Make sure pop-ups are allowed in your browser
- **Statement not parsing** → Try the CSV format instead of PDF
- **Report taking too long** → Add at least a few expenses first, then generate
- **UPI payment not opening** → Make sure GPay/PhonePe is installed on your phone
